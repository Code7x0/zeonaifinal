Rules for every clip
Loops: muted, under 30 seconds, a still image shown first, loaded only when scrolled
into view.
Longer videos: click to play, with captions. Never autoplay with sound.
Content: sample data only. No customer names or figures visible in any recording.
Consistency: the same browser frame, zoom level and cursor style across all clips, at
16:9.
Accessibility: loops pause when the visitor has reduced motion switched on.
Where not to add video
Trust, security and legal pages: readers there want text they can quote.
Architecture diagrams: the animated diagrams already do the job.
Backgrounds: no stock or abstract footage anywhere; it undercuts the real-product
message.
