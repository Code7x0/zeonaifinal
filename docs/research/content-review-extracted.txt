The five biggest problems
Internal notes are live on the page. About 15 pages carry lines written for the copywriter, not the buyer. The full list is in the next section.
Three product naming systems. The platform is "AI Workspace". /products is headed "DeepDelve family" and lists "DeepDelve® DeepProbe", InvoiceAI, CashfloAI and DocAI. Mock-ups say "DeepProbe · Ask AI". Decided: DeepDelve becomes Agent Hub and DeepProbe becomes AI Workspace everywhere; see the naming section below.
The site sells by saying what it is not. "Not slideware", "not bolted on", "not a universal plug-and-play claim", "not a guarantee", "not a performance claim". The tone is defensive and undersells the product.
Agents are buried. ZeonAI builds agents for medium to large enterprises, but the hero headline does not mention agents and Agent Hub is module 09 of 9.
Proof is anonymous and does not match the logos. All four case studies are unnamed. The logo strip shows ten organisations; the October brief lists a different client set (Telikom PNG, PMO PNG, Post-Courier, HL Landscapes, Orthocell, Paladin), none of which appear.
Naming: DeepDelve becomes Agent Hub, DeepProbe becomes AI Workspace
Replace "DeepDelve" with "Agent Hub" everywhere it appears. It occurs 15 times across six pages, plus the header menu. Drop the ® unless "Agent Hub" is a registered mark.

Rules for the designer:
One treatment: all third-party logos in a single grey, equal height, with colour only on hover.
Official files only: take each logo from the vendor&apos;s brand page and follow its usage rules.
Only what is true: show a connector logo only if that connector works today, and a certification mark only if it is held.
No model-vendor logos: the brief says to stay model-agnostic.
