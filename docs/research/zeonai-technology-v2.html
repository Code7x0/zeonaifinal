02 · Retrieval

Retrieval that reads the whole document first.

Most pipelines cut a document into passages and index each one alone. A line like “this reduced churn by 18%” then loses what “this” was. We index every passage with the document around it still in view.

Read the engineering note →

Indexed in context

Answer

The loyalty pricing change reduced churn by 18% in the second half.[1] It was approved by the pricing committee in March.[2]

04 · AI-assisted determinism

Probabilistic where it helps. Deterministic where it counts.

11 · Rule builder

Rules written by an agent. Proved before they run.

Reconciliation lives in its rules: does this invoice match that order, within what tolerance, and what happens when it does not. An analyst describes the check in plain language. An agent drafts the rule, tests it and shows its working. Nothing changes until the analyst applies it.
